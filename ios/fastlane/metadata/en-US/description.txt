How much electricity did you use this year? Will your instalments cover the bill? Kostenkurve keeps meter readings, contracts and fixed costs in one place – and shows you where the curve is heading.

USAGE & COSTS AT A GLANCE
• Log meter readings, prices and amounts – Kostenkurve works out usage and costs day by day, even when prices change
• A 12-month history for every meter, compared with last year
• Insights: costs per month and category, year-over-year comparison

TEMPLATES, YOUR OWN FIELDS & FORMULAS
• Eight templates to get started: electricity, heating, water, rent & utilities, car, internet & mobile, insurance, subscriptions
• Fully customisable: your own categories, fields (number, amount, percent, date, choice …), units and conversions – e.g. gas from m³ to kWh
• Formula fields do the maths, e.g. litres per 100 km

FORECAST WITH A RANGE
• Next year's forecast from your usage, current prices and announced price increases
• A range instead of false precision – and what it is made of

INSTALMENT CHECK
• Will your instalments do? See the expected credit or back payment – and which instalment would fit

SHARE WITH YOUR PARTNER
• Share individual categories such as household or car – via code, link or QR code, to record together or view only

REMINDERS
• For meter readings and key dates such as the end of a contract

WORKS OFFLINE
• Meter in the basement without signal? Entries are saved and sent once you are back online

PRIVACY
• Get started without signing up, no ads, no trackers
• Locally on your iPhone – or in the cloud on servers in the EU
• Delete your account in the app at any time

KOSTENKURVE CLOUD
Kostenkurve is free and fully usable as long as your data stays on your iPhone. The optional Kostenkurve Cloud subscription keeps it backed up in the cloud and on every device you sign in on; it also lets you share categories with your partner and attach receipts – photos and PDFs such as bills, meter photos or contracts, with 5 GB of storage. It renews monthly; cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings.

Amounts are shown in euros.
