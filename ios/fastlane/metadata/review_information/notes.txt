No login and no demo account are needed. On first launch Kostenkurve signs in automatically with an anonymous account, so every feature can be tried right away.

Suggested path (labels in English when the device language is English; German is the primary language):
1. On first launch a short welcome lets you pick templates (Electricity, Heating and Water are preselected) → "Create 3 categories" → reminders "Allow" or "Later". Later on, more categories via the Categories tab → "+".
2. Open the category and add an entry, e.g. a meter reading → "Save entry". Add a second reading with a later date to see usage, costs and the history chart.
3. Insights tab: costs per month, year-over-year comparison, forecast with a range and the instalment check.

Sharing a category (category → Share) requires a secured account: Settings → Account → "Secure with Apple" or "Secure with email" (one-time code). The partner joins with the invite code, link or QR code via "Accept invite".

Kostenkurve Plus is an optional auto-renewable subscription (monthly or yearly). It only unlocks attachments (photos and PDFs on entries, 5 GB storage); everything else is free. It can be purchased with a Sandbox account, and "Restore Purchases" is on the subscription screen.

The account can be deleted in the app under Settings → Account.
